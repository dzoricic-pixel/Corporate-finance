Historical Summary of Steve Jobs' Stake in Apple:
  • 1980 (IPO): Held ~15% (7.5 million shares), worth ~$217 million at IPO, diluted to ~12% shortly after.
  • 1985 (Ouster): After being forced out by John Sculley and the Board, Jobs sold all of his ~11% stake (~$130M value), keeping only 1 single share so he could receive financial reports and attend shareholder meetings.
  • 1997 (Return): Upon Apple's $400M acquisition of NeXT, Jobs received 1.5 million shares. In mid-1997, frustrated with CEO Gil Amelio, Jobs sold all 1.5M shares (causing a stock dip) before taking over as interim CEO later that year.
  • 2000–2011 (iCEO & Return): Over time, Jobs accepted a $1 annual salary and was granted stock options/RSUs (including a stock grant in 2000). At his passing in October 2011, Jobs owned approximately 5.5 million shares (~0.6% of Apple), valued at ~$2.1B at the time. (His largest holding was actually a ~7.7% stake in Disney from the Pixar acquisition).
